Is pay keeping up with prices?

Real pay

Pay ÷ consumer prices, index (2019 = 100). Above 100, a pay packet buys more than in 2019.

US: average hourly earnings of production and nonsupervisory workers ÷ CPI (BLS via FRED). UK: average weekly earnings ÷ CPI (ONS). Euro area: labour cost index, wages and salaries ÷ HICP, quarterly from 2009 (Eurostat). The US jump in 2020 is a composition effect: low-paid jobs went first.

Inflation

Consumer prices, change over 12 months (%).

US CPI (BLS via FRED), UK CPI (ONS), euro-area HICP (Eurostat via FRED); 12-month log changes.

The cost of a mortgage

Repayment per 100,000 borrowed

Monthly repayment as a share of a month's average full-time pay (%). US: 30-year fixed; UK: over 25 years at each quoted rate.

Rates: Freddie Mac 30-year (via FRED); Bank of England quoted 2- and 5-year fixed (75% LTV) and revert-to rates. Pay as for real pay.

Repaying a typical home

Monthly repayment on a typical home as a share of income (%). US: 80% loan on the median price against median household income. UK: 75% loan on the average price against one average earner.

US median sales price and median household income (Census via FRED); UK HPI average price (HM Land Registry).

The 2026 Hormuz closure

Real pay under the closure scenarios

Model output, not data

Change in real pay (%) from February 2026 for each loss of Hormuz flow held for 12 months (points of February's strait flow), and for 2026 as the IEA counts it month by month. Bars are 90% bands, ticks the medians, the dashed line what happened.

What happened: —

Consumer-price fans from the knock-on chain (notes/chain-scenarios-3m.csv and notes/chain-scenarios-2026-3m.csv: Lévy oil paths with the flow premium per point of the IEA's lost flow, then the Bayes and neural-network ensemble), fitted on data to January 2026. Nominal pay keeps its pace of the year before, so the band is the price band. The 2026 bar uses the IEA's monthly loss (89 points in March to 47 in September); the flow link was fitted to that event, so that bar is in sample.
