# Bayesian oil pass-through (MCMC): results

_Generated by `experiments/macro_bayes.py report` on 2026-10-24 from 8 forward-chaining folds (2008-01..2025-10, 214 forecast origins), the same folds and data as `notes/macro-nn-results.md` (inputs: `notes/macro-panel.md`, fetched 2026-10-24). The data are real and revised (not real-time vintages). The only simulated inputs are the forecast mode's oil paths: a block bootstrap of training months, scaled to the CBOE OVX implied volatility (FRED OVXCLS) and centred on the WTI futures curve slope (EIA RCLC1-4, to 2024-04), fetched 2026-10-24._

Skill = 1 − CRPS / CRPS of the v1 climatology (training mean and spread), averaged over the outputs in the group; 0 is no better than climatology. **What-if** forecasts are given the oil path that happened; **forecast** knows nothing after the origin. Variants: **Bayes** (volatility follows oil), **+own-vol** (plus each series' own recent volatility), **+CDV** (plus a drifting volatility per target group). Every column is scored on the same folds and origins.

**Main model: Bayes+own-vol** (the converged variant with the lowest CRPS over all outputs, what-if).
**Not shown: Bayes+CDV** did not converge (or did not run) on fold(s) [3, 6, 7]; see Sampler health. Run it with more samples on Iridis (`cluster/iridis/macro_bayes.slurm`).
