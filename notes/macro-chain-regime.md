**Keep rule** (fixed before scoring): no target group worse beyond 2 Newey–West errors on the folds (rec_regime minus rec, what-if and levy), and the replay improves in its deciding mode, lost 82 with D from the scenario: lower mean scaled CRPS and coverage no lower.

## Verdict

**Passes the keep rule.** No group is worse on the folds, and the replay improves.

**Decision:** Not adopted (2026-10-25): the recommended ensemble stays `rec`. The rule's pass is narrow and comes from the mixture's spread; inflation, the app's headline, is worse in the scenario run.

**Reviewer's checks** (the cost-of-living session; reported, not part of the rule): lost 82, inflation: CRPS rec_regime − rec +0.068 ✗; what-if, inflation: -0.010, same sign as lost 82 ✗; lost 82, cost: CRPS rec_regime − rec -0.022 ✓; what-if, cost: -0.062, same sign as lost 82 ✓; lost 82, coverage 97% against 97% ✓. **They disagree with the verdict.**

**The member alone:** in the replay bayes_regime scores whatif 0.647 against bayes_own's 0.641; lost 82 0.685 against bayes_own's 0.602. Inside the ensemble the what-if moves from 0.636 to 0.598 with coverage 89% → 94%: where the member alone is worse, the ensemble's gain comes from the mixture's wider spread, not a sharper member.

**How much evidence:** only fold(s) [7] have a disruption in training, so on the other folds bayes_regime is bayes_own and the fold comparison is a no-harm check; 8 what-if origins and 0 forecast origins there have D = 1 ahead. The replay is one origin. Both are thin evidence either way: κ and ζ are learned from the 10 months of 2022 alone.
