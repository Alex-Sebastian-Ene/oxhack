Outcomes are real and revised (not real-time vintages). Simulated: the oil paths of the two forecast modes, and every model's draws._

Modes: **whatif** is given the oil path that happened; **levy** and **bootstrap** know nothing after the origin, with oil paths from GARCH-CGMY (`oil_paths.py`) or the block bootstrap. Members: `bayes_own`, `global_lin`, `global`, `global_tcn`. **equal**: equal-weight mixture; **forward**: per group, the weights (simplex grid, step 0.25) with the lowest CRPS on earlier folds (equal for fold 0).
