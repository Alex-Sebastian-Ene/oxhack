# Polyethylene: production proxy, feedstocks, import origins (research desk, 24 Oct 2026)

Data for the materials globe: `polyethylene.json` (one source per row).

**How collected:** web search on 24 Oct 2026, so **every figure is from search-result snippets** of
the cited pages. Check the headline numbers against the primary sources before the pitch.

## Production (proxy)

No country table of polyethylene (PE) output alone was found. The globe uses each country's share of
world output of the four big plastics together (PE, PP, PET and PS, 2024; Eunomia figures reported at
the UN plastics talks):
- China 34%, US 13%, Saudi Arabia 5%, South Korea 5%, India 4%, Japan 3%, Germany 2%.
- China's ethylene capacity passed 62 Mt/yr at the end of 2025, about 25% of the world (SunSirs).

## Ingredients

- **Naphtha** feeds most crackers in Asia and Europe; **ethane** feeds those in the US and the Gulf.
  Producer shares are unconfirmed, so the globe shows their exporters instead (see `exports.md`).
- **South Korea's naphtha imports (H1 2026, KITA):** Algeria 17%, UAE 15%, US 13%, India 10%, Oman 9%.
- **China's PE imports (2025, by value):** UAE 21% and Saudi Arabia 13% (Q3); South Korea 11% (Q3);
  US 11% (October only; 18% in Q1); Iran 8% (customs, the exact page unconfirmed).

## Oil and Hormuz exposure

- The Middle East holds about 15% of world PE capacity, and about 84% of that ships out through
  Hormuz (S&P). The region exported over 12.5 Mt of PE in 2025 (ICIS).
- In 2025, Hormuz carried about half of Korea's naphtha imports, about 70% of Japan's and about 40%
  of China's (Kpler via Energy Intelligence).
- **2026:** China's PE imports fell 24% in Jan–May, to a ten-year monthly low in May, while its
  exports rose 247%. Gulf naphtha to Asia fell from 1.4 mb/d in Jan–Feb to 160 kb/d in March (Kpler).
- **Price link:** the globe uses Seb's Bayesian response for the US plastics resin PPI (+2.3% at 12
  months per +10% Brent). Its band is the uncertainty of the average effect only.
