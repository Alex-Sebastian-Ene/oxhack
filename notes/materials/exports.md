# Exports and the biggest trade flows (research desk, 24 Oct 2026)

Data for the globe's "Shipped" view: `exports.json` (one source per row).

**How collected:** web search on 24 Oct 2026. Every primary site (OEC, worldsteel, USGS, Comtrade,
Energy Institute) was blocked, so **all figures are from search-result snippets**. The search
budget ran out before **bauxite, alumina, urea, polyethylene and naphtha**. Those five are left
off the map until researched, rather than shown from guesses.

Where no world total was found for the same year (steel, scrap, crude, aluminium), `share` is
empty and the globe shows volumes or values instead of a share of world exports.

Headlines:
- **Iron ore (2025, seaborne):**
  - Exporters: Australia 55%, Brazil 23%.
  - Flows: Australia → China 831 Mt, about 85% of Australian exports.
- **Coking coal (2024):**
  - Exporters: Australia 42%, Mongolia 15%, US 14%, Russia 13%.
  - Flows: Mongolia → China, 46% of China's imports.
- **Scrap (2025):**
  - Exporters: EU 16.7 Mt, US 11.8 Mt, Japan 7.7 Mt.
  - Flows: US → Türkiye 3.6 Mt.
- **Jet fuel (2025, seaborne, ~1.77 mb/d):**
  - Exporters: Kuwait ~15%, South Korea ~14%.
  - Flows: Korea supplies about 86% of US West Coast imports.
  - 2026: the Hormuz closure removed about 21% of seaborne jet supply (Kpler).
- **Crude (2024):**
  - Exporters: Saudi Arabia 321 Mt, Russia 243, Canada 219, US 198, UAE 183, Iraq 180.
  - 2026: Gulf exports fell from 24 to 8.7 mb/d in March 2026 (IEA).
- **LNG (2025):**
  - Exporters: US 25%, Qatar 19%, Australia 18%.
  - 2026: Hormuz cut about 20% of world LNG supply.
- **Primary aluminium (2024, by value):** Canada, UAE and Russia lead. Canada sends about 91% to the US.
