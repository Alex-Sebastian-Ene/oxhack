# Aluminium and nitrogen fertiliser (research desk, 24 Oct 2026)

Data for the materials globe: `aluminium.json`, `fertiliser.json` (one source per row).

**How collected:** web search on 24 Oct 2026. USGS, IAI, Comtrade, OEC, IFA and IEA pages were
blocked, so most figures are from **search-result snippets**. Two sources were opened in full:
USGS monthly reports through their S3 mirror, and the Energy Institute gas data through OWID on
GitHub. Polyethylene is **not** on the globe yet: its figures could not be verified, and a
follow-up search is running.

## Aluminium (2025, USGS MCS 2026; world 74 Mt)

- **Producers:** China 45 Mt (61%), India 4.2, Russia 3.9, Canada 3.3, UAE 2.7, Bahrain 1.6.
- **Bauxite (world ~440 Mt):** Guinea 34%, Australia 22%, China 20%, Brazil 7.5%.
- **Alumina:** China 61% of 145 Mt metallurgical grade.
- **China's bauxite imports (200.5 Mt):** Guinea 74%, Australia 19%. Imports are about 70% of supply.
- **Canada's alumina:** Brazil 82% by value (2022).
- **Rusal:** its own refineries cover about 70% of its alumina; China supplies about 25% (derived).
- **2026:** Iranian strikes on 28 Mar hit Alba (Bahrain) and EGA Al Taweelah (UAE). LME aluminium reached a four-year high of $3,787.5/t in early June.

## Nitrogen fertiliser (ammonia, kt N, 2025)

- **Producers:** China 49,000, India 15,000, Russia 15,000, US 14,000, Indonesia 6,000, Saudi Arabia 5,200, Iran 4,800, Egypt, Canada, Trinidad, Qatar.
- **Caveat on shares:** they use an assumed 150,000 kt world total. USGS gives 145,000 for 2022, and the 2025 total is unconfirmed.
- **Gas (2024, Energy Institute):** US 25%, Russia 15%, Iran 6.4%, China 6%, Canada 4.7%, Qatar 4.4%.
- **Where urea imports come from:**
  - India: Oman 46%, Russia 16%, Saudi Arabia 9%.
  - US: Russia 36%, Qatar 22%, Canada 11%.
  - Brazil: Oman, Qatar and Nigeria about 18% each (2024).
- **2026:** the Gulf was 36% of world urea exports (IFPRI), and Gulf shipments are still largely halted. China banned exports in March, then allowed about 1.5 Mt of quotas.
