_Generated by `experiments/oil_chain.py cv` on 2026-10-24. Same FRED series and link definitions as `notes/oil-chain-links.md` (fetched 2026-10-24). Nothing here is synthetic._

The single split in `notes/oil-chain-links.md` (train 1991–2008, test 2009–2026) depends on where the cut falls. Here each link's whole window is cut into k contiguous blocks, for every k from 8 to 16, which gives 108 folds per link.

- **Blocked:** each block is held out once, and the link is fitted on all the other blocks. A gap the length of the link's lags is left either side of the block, so lagged regressors cannot straddle the cut.
- **Forward:** each block is predicted only from the blocks before it. Blocks with under 3 years of past data are skipped. This is the time-honest version.
- **R²** is out of sample, against the training mean. **Pooled R²** combines all the folds for one k. **Fold effects** are the link's summed lag coefficient in each fold, in the units of `notes/oil-chain-links.md`.
