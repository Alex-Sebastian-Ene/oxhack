Each link regresses the monthly change of the downstream series on the current and lagged monthly change of the upstream one (log changes for prices, level changes for rates). The effect is the sum of the lag coefficients, with a Newey-West ± (1 standard error). Fitted on the train years, then used unchanged on the test years: **out-of-sample R² > 0** means the link predicts better than a constant there. "Test-years refit" re-estimates on the test years alone, to show whether the size of the link has drifted.
