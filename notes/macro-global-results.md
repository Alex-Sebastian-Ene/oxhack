# Global one-step network: results

_Generated by `experiments/macro_global.py report` on 2026-10-24 from 8 forward-chaining folds (2008-01..2025-10, 214 forecast origins), the same folds, training months and data as `notes/macro-bayes-results.md` (inputs: `notes/macro-panel.md`). The data are real and revised (not real-time vintages); nothing is synthetic. Every forecast here is a **what-if**: it is given the oil path that happened. `runs/macro/bayes-own-fold-*.json` generated 2026-10-24T20:17:30+00:00 to 2026-10-24T20:27:01+00:00. `runs/macro/bayes-fold-*.json` generated 2026-10-24T19:59:02+00:00 to 2026-10-24T20:05:23+00:00._

Skill = 1 − CRPS / CRPS of the v1 climatology (training mean and spread), averaged over the outputs in the group; 0 is no better than climatology. `global` is one network shared by all 22 series; `global_lin` adds a per-series linear term (momentum and oil lags) to its location. `OLS, same inputs` is per-series least squares on the networks' inputs (12 own lags, oil lags 0–12, volatility state) with Gaussian errors, simulated the same way: the inputs without the network. `Bayes own-vol` is the main Bayesian model (`macro_bayes.py --vol own`: each series' own recent volatility sets its band); `Bayes oil-vol` lets the band follow oil volatility only (`--vol oil`).
