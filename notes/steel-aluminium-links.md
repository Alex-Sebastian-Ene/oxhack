# Oil → steel and oil → aluminium price links (OXH-5)

_Generated by `experiments/metal_links.py` on 2026-10-24. Every series is from FRED (St. Louis Fed), monthly averages; fetch dates and file hashes are in the data table below. Nothing here is synthetic._

**Method.** The same link as the OXH-3 table and `macro_nn.Anchor`: the monthly log change of the price on the current and lagged monthly log change of Brent (lags 0–12), months from 2001-02, Newey–West errors (lag 12) on the summed effect. Checked on macro_nn's 8 forward-chaining folds, each fitted only on the months before its test block (test years 2008-01..2025-10). A fold validates the link if its summed effect (lags 0–12) clears 2 Newey–West errors there. The numbers shown come from the fit on all months.

- **The shock:** a sustained +10% Brent move, i.e. the price stays 10% higher from next month on.
- **After 3 / 12 months:** ln 1.10 times the sum of the first 3 / 12 lag coefficients, in % of the price level.
- **±:** one Newey–West standard error. In the CSV, q05/q95 are the mean ± 1.645 errors: the uncertainty of the average effect, not the range of single outcomes (which is wider).
- **Validated:** the all-data fit clears 2 errors and at least 6 of 8 folds do (the weakest row the OXH-3 table called validated cleared 6/8). Anything else is **not validated**.
- **Other move sizes:** the link is linear in log terms, so scale by ln(1+x) / ln(1.10); a 10% fall gives the mirror image (rises and falls were not tested separately).

**Series choice.**

- **Steel:** `WPU1017` (steel mill products) is complete from 2000. Scrap (`WPU1012`) is added because it was cheap; it is far more volatile (monthly sd about 8% against 3% for mill products).
- **Aluminium:** on FRED (series pages checked 2026-10-24), `WPU102501` is **aluminum mill shapes**, complete from 2000. OXH-5 named `WPU102502` for mill shapes, but that code is **copper and brass mill shapes**, so it is not used. The primary-aluminium PPI (`PCU331313331313`, alumina refining and primary aluminum production) stops in 2019-03 and has 25 missing months (2010-01..2011-11, 2016-12..2017-01), so it is not clean enough.
- **World price check:** OXH-5 asked for LME aluminium. The closest free monthly series on FRED is the IMF's global aluminium benchmark (`PALUMUSDM`, $/t, period averages), used here in its place.

**Caveats.**

- These are average historical pass-throughs, mixing supply-driven and demand-driven oil moves (see the sensitivity table).
- The PPIs are US prices of mill products (rolled and drawn steel and aluminium), not world crude steel or primary aluminium. US steel and aluminium prices also move with trade policy (Section 232 tariffs from 2018, raised again in 2025), which this link does not model.
- In the 2026 closure, aluminium also took a direct hit: strikes on Gulf smelters on 28 Mar 2026 (`notes/materials/aluminium-fertiliser.md`). The closure table mixes that with the oil effect.
