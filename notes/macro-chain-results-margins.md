# The knock-on chain end to end: Lévy oil paths and the model ensemble

_Generated by `experiments/macro_chain.py report` on 2026-10-24 from 8 forward-chaining folds (2008-01..2025-10, 214 forecast origins), the folds and data of `notes/macro-bayes-results.md` (inputs: `notes/macro-panel.md`; daily Brent FRED DCOILBRENTEU fetched 2026-10-24). Outcomes are real and revised (not real-time vintages). Simulated: the oil paths of the two forecast modes, and every model's draws._

Skill = 1 − CRPS / CRPS of the v1 climatology, averaged over the outputs in the group; 0 is no better than climatology. Modes: **whatif** is given the oil path that happened; **levy** and **bootstrap** know nothing after the origin, with oil paths from GARCH-CGMY (`oil_paths.py`) or the block bootstrap. Members: `bayes_own`, `global_lin`, `global`. **equal**: equal-weight mixture; **forward**: per group, the weights (simplex grid, step 0.25) with the lowest CRPS on earlier folds (equal for fold 0). **With the margin member** (`experiments/margins.py`, `runs/macro/chain-J-margins.npz`): every mixture of two or more members takes it as one more equal-weight member on the three fuel targets' columns (diesel, jet fuel, refinery PPI; cost group); single-member columns are the members alone.
