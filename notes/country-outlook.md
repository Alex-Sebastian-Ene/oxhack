# Country inflation outlook

_Generated by `experiments/country_outlook.py` on 2026-10-25. Nothing here is synthetic._

Sources: FAOSTAT consumer price indices (general and food, 2015 = 100, monthly; https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/CP), fetched 2026-10-25; Brent: FRED MCOILBRENTEU (monthly average, US$), fetched 2026-10-25.

Method: one model per price kind and horizon, pooled across countries, predicting how far the next h months' annualised log rate departs from the last 12 months'; candidates ridge, median (LAD) linear, gradient-boosted trees (squared loss, clipped target) and the LAD/trees average; rolling-origin backtest every 3 months, linear models refitted at each origin and trees every 6 months, on outcomes known by then; the best candidate per kind and horizon is used, naive where none beats it; 90% band = forecast + pooled 5%/95% quantiles of error / (the country's own 36-month volatility of seasonally adjusted monthly changes x sqrt h).

Features, all known at the origin month: last month's rate, annualised, minus the 12-month rate; the same for 3 months; the same for 6 months; the 12-month rate; the country's own 5-year mean rate minus its 12-month rate; the cross-country median 12-month rate minus the country's; own minus partner 12-month rate (food minus all, all minus food); Brent's 1-month log change; Brent's 3-month log change; Brent's 12-month log change; the country's usual seasonal departure over the h months ahead (last five years); month of year (sine); month of year (cosine).

Caveats: prices are FAOSTAT's current vintage, not what was published at each origin, so the backtest flatters every rule a little (naive included). Forecasts start at each country's latest price month and only use Brent up to that month; Brent's moves after it are not in them, so add the oil link on top for a scenario. Bands for the six fragile countries and for inflation above 50% come from the naive rule's errors elsewhere and under-cover there (see the fragile column).

## Backtest

Skill = 1 - MAE(model) / MAE(naive) on the h-month % change, pooled over countries and origins every 3 months from 2015-01 to 2024-10; above zero beats naive. Naive: the latest 12-month rate continues. Lebanon, Sudan, South Sudan, Syria, Venezuela and Zimbabwe are never trained on and get the naive rule; so does any country-month with 12-month inflation above 50% (or below -33%). Those rows are scored apart (the fragile column), not in the pooled skill.
