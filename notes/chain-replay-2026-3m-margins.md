_Generated by `experiments/macro_chain.py replay` on 2026-10-25. Every model is fitted on months up to 2026-01 only. Members: bayes_own, global_lin, global, mixed with equal weights, the best ensemble on the 8 folds (`notes/macro-chain-results.md`). Plus the margin member on the three fuel targets (`experiments/margins.py`: product margins fitted on the same months, crisis term from the IEA actions before 2026, so 2026 is out of sample for it; `notes/margins-results.md`). Simulated: the oil paths and the model draws._

- **what-if (out of sample):** the chain is given the Brent path that happened. This tests the knock-on layer on an event it never saw.
- **lost 0:** a genuine forecast with GARCH-CGMY oil paths and no scenario: what a forecaster without the flow link would have said.
- **IEA 2026:** GARCH-CGMY paths plus the flow scenario of 2026 as the IEA counts it: 89, 84, 85, 55, 62, 65, 47 points of February's strait flow lost in 2026-03..2026-09 (`notes/hormuz-iea-flows.csv`; later months hold the last). **In sample for the flow link:** its premium curve (`notes/flow-premium-iea.csv`, per point of the IEA's lost flow, each path drawn within the curve's 90% band) was fitted to this same event; Bab el-Mandeb 2023-24 checks only its sign and the role of rerouting out of sample (`notes/flow-events.md`, `notes/hormuz-flow-check.md`). So it shows how the chain composes, not that the flow link forecasts.
