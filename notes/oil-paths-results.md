# Lévy oil paths: backtest of the oil layer

_Generated by `experiments/oil_paths.py check` on 2026-10-24. Daily Brent: FRED DCOILBRENTEU (EIA), `data/raw/macro/fred_DCOILBRENTEU.csv` fetched 2026-10-24; its monthly means reproduce the knock-on panel's Brent changes (largest difference 0.0e+00). 214 forecast origins (2008-01..2025-10) on the 8 folds of `notes/macro-bayes-results.md`. GARCH-Lévy parameters: runs/levy/rolling-2008.json .. runs/levy/rolling-2025.json, each fitted on daily returns before 1 January of the origin's year. Futures drift: EIA WTI RCLC1-4 (to 2024-04) for every model. The paths are simulated; the outcomes are real. 400 paths per origin._

Target: Brent's log change (%, ×100) of the monthly mean price from the origin month to h months later, the input the knock-on models take. CRPS is in percentage points (lower is better). `bootstrap` is the forecast mode of `macro_bayes.py`: 3-month blocks of training months, scaled to the OVX implied volatility at the origin.
