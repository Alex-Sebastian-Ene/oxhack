**How to read it.** Log score = mean log density of the realised test return; higher is better, and differences are in nats per observation. ± is a Newey-West standard error (lag 10 daily, lag h for h-day windows): a difference within about twice its ± is not distinguishable from zero. "Without Mar-May 2020" drops the months when WTI went negative and Brent fell 64% in a day (2020-04-21), which on its own moves the Gaussian's mean score by about 0.07. Tail columns give the % of test days below the model's 1% and 5% quantiles and above its 95% and 99% quantiles; a calibrated model shows 1 / 5 / 5 / 1. Clipped counts days where the Fourier-series density fell below 1e-12.
